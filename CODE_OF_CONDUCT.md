# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Agent Systems Fieldbook a respectful, evidence-oriented experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

The project welcomes strong technical disagreement. It does not accept personal attacks, harassment, deception, unattributed appropriation, or attempts to manipulate contributors or automated reviewers.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- grounding claims in inspectable evidence;
- distinguishing facts, inferences, and unknowns;
- giving complete attribution and correcting it promptly;
- providing specific, actionable review feedback;
- disclosing conflicts of interest and promotional relationships;
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and source licenses;
- accepting a rejection when the evidence or rights are insufficient;
- helping contributors improve an entry without lowering the quality bar.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct;
- trolling, insults, or sustained disruption;
- doxxing or publishing private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting fabricated evidence, fake attribution, or undisclosed generated content;
- embedding prompt injections, credential requests, malicious links, or executable payloads in contributions;
- pressuring maintainers to bypass evaluation, licensing, or safety controls;
- using the project primarily for spam, reputation laundering, or product promotion;
- retaliating against good-faith security or quality reports.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may edit, reject, remove, or lock contributions and discussions that violate this code, and will explain moderation decisions when practical and safe.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when an individual is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a private communication path through the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Do not publish sensitive personal details in an issue.

For security-related conduct or malicious submissions, follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply the following responses depending on impact and pattern:

1. **Correction:** private or public clarification and a request to change behavior.
2. **Warning:** formal notice with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction:** time-limited loss of interaction or contribution privileges.
4. **Permanent restriction:** permanent removal from project spaces.
