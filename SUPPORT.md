# Support

## Questions about using the fieldbook

Open a GitHub Discussion when discussions are enabled. Otherwise, open a narrowly scoped issue describing:

- the job you are trying to accomplish;
- the artifact or taxonomy area involved;
- what you already inspected;
- the specific uncertainty.

## Candidate and correction requests

Use the issue forms under `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`.

## Security

Follow `SECURITY.md`. Do not disclose secrets, exploit details, malicious payloads, or private data in a public issue.

## What maintainers cannot provide

The fieldbook does not certify that an artifact is safe for every environment, guarantee model behavior, provide legal advice about third-party licenses, or replace product-specific documentation. Users remain responsible for verifying current product behavior and adapting permissions and guardrails.
